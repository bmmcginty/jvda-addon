# JVDA command tables

JAWSKey means the configured NVDA modifier (Insert, Numpad Insert, or CapsLock).
Bare review keys are active only in their applicable JVDA cursor mode.

## Layered commands (JAWSKey+space)

Press JAWSKey+space, then the keys in the Key column. Two rising
tones mean the layer is open. The basic layer closes after one
command; a sublayer stays open until Escape, Space, Tab, Enter or
two seconds of silence. Question mark lists the open layer's own
keys.

Two falling tones mean the layer has closed with nothing else to
say, after Escape or a timeout. A command that speaks its result or
opens a window plays no tone: the answer itself is the signal that
the layer has ended.

A key with a JAWS command JVDA cannot provide reports "command
invalid"; a key with no JAWS command reports "command unassigned".
Both end the layer. A JAWS sublayer JVDA cannot support is not
offered as a layer at all: its key reports "command invalid"
rather than opening a layer in which every key would.

The layer takes JAWSKey+space from NVDA's browse and focus mode toggle.
JAWSKey+Z is JVDA's Virtual PC Cursor toggle and does the same job.
Switch the layer off in NVDA menu, Preferences, Settings, JVDA to
give JAWSKey+space back to NVDA.
